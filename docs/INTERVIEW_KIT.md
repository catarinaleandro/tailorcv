# Interview kit (3 to 5 people, 15 minutes each)

1. Walk me through how you tailored your last CV. How long did it take?
2. What is the slowest or most annoying part?
3. Have you used an AI tool for this? What went wrong?
4. What would you never let AI write for you?
5. If a tool asked you questions before building your CV, what would make you trust it or skip it?

Record in eval/interviews.md: finding, number of people who said it, and the design decision it drove.
